# Bengali AI language evaluation portfolio

## Portfolio status

This is a completed personal portfolio project. It demonstrates Bengali transcription review, audio-language evaluation, and rubric-based review of Bengali AI text. The work is not presented as client or paid experience.

## What is included

### Transcription review

- Compared an ElevenLabs machine transcript of `clip_01` against a user-provided verified Bengali reference transcript.
- Documented 18 evidence-based, transcript-order differences in `error_analysis.csv`.
- Preserved the supplied reference transcript conventions, including fillers, restarts, and spelling forms.
- Left timestamp columns blank rather than infer timing that was not supplied or verified.

### Audio language evaluation

- Reviewed two Bengali speech samples using the same four categories: fluency, pronunciation, intonation, and intelligibility/clarity.
- `clip_01`: identified frequent pauses and fillers, uneven intonation, and reduced clarity; the detailed English review is in `clip_01_audio_evaluation.md`.
- `clip_02`: identified several pronunciation concerns and documented the copyright licence and attribution requirements for the Wikimedia Commons source.
- Did not add unverified timestamps or claim word-level evidence that was not documented.

### Bengali text QA

Reviewed six synthetic portfolio samples using a consistent verdict and correction workflow:

1. Customer-support reply: tone, unsupported promises, and next-step clarity.
2. Formal academic email: email structure and formal register.
3. Translation and localization: natural Bengali and unnecessary English mixing.
4. Fact-checking: incorrect dates and overly narrow framing, verified against UNESCO.
5. Safety: unsafe banking/phishing guidance.
6. Audience and instruction-following: child-friendly wording, language choice, and length.

Every case preserves the original answer, records the review findings, supplies a corrected Bengali answer, and includes an English rationale.
